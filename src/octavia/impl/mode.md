# MIDI modes
Octavia is compatible with a range of modes on MIDI synthesizers. A list of supported modes to their respective keys is available below.

## Internal
- `?`: The default "nothing" mode. Octavia will try to detect the correct mode.

## Pre-GM
- `doc`: Yamaha DOC. DOC is short for Disk Orchestra Collection.
- `mt32`: Roland MT-32.
- `qy10`: Yamaha QY10 native.
- `qy20`: Yamaha QY20 native.

## GM 1
- `gm`: General MIDI.
- `05rw`: Korg 05R/W and Korg X5. Compatible with AG-10.
- `cs2x`: Yamaha CS2x. Compatible with CS1x.
- `cs6x`: Yamaha CS6x.
- `gs`: Roland GS. GS is short for General Sound.
- `gus`: Gravis UltraSound.
- `k11`: Kawai GMega or K11.
- `motif`: Yamaha Motif ES.
- `ns5r`: Korg NS5R. Compatible with NX5R, and has limited compatibility with Korg N1R and N5.
- `s90es`: Yamaha S90 ES.
- `sc`: Roland GS mode, but with mode 1 or mode 2 set. Specific to Roland SoundCanvas SC-88 and SC-88 Pro.
- `sg`: Akai SG.
- `x5d`: Korg X5D(R). Compatible with AG-10.
- `xg`: Yamaha XG. Compatible with TG-100 and TG-300. XG is short for eXtended General.

## GM 2
- `g2`: General MIDI Level 2.
- `krs`: Korg KROSS 2.
- `pa`: Korg PA. Targets PA50SD, PA80, PA1x, MicroArranger and equivalents.
- `rhc`: Roland HyperCanvas.
- `sd`: Roland SD. Targets SD-20, SD-80 and SD-90. SD is used for Roland's Studio Canvas lineup.
